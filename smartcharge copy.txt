SmartCharge AI is an intelligent EV charging recommendation platform.

It recommends the best charging station based on several factors.

The recommendation engine considers battery percentage, current location,
destination, distance to the charging station, charging speed, charging cost,
station availability, station reliability, and route optimization.

The system can estimate whether a charging station is reachable based on the
vehicle's remaining range and the distance to the station.

It can also estimate charging time and charging cost.

The goal of SmartCharge AI is not simply to find charging stations.
It answers the question: Where should the EV driver charge, and why?
